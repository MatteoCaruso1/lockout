Lockout: Strict App Blocker for iPhone

Pick a length, and every app and website on your iPhone is blocked until the time is up. Nobody can end a lock early, including you. Phone and emergency calls always work.

  • Lock for 1 to 12 hours, or set schedules such as every night from 10 PM to 7 AM.
  • No accounts, no internet connection, no data collected. Everything stays on your iPhone.
  • Built on Apple's Screen Time, so locks keep running after a restart and end on time, even if the app is closed.
