Privacy policy

Lockout: Strict App Blocker for iPhone

Last updated 4 November 2026

In short: Lockout collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers, and it never connects to the internet.

What the app stores

Your lock settings (lock times, schedules, a history of locks) and, if you turn on Strict mode, the Screen Time passcode it generated for you. All of this is kept only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

Screen Time

Lockout uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to block apps and websites during a lock. Apple does not give Lockout the names of your apps or your browsing; it only receives anonymous tokens that stay on the device. Lockout cannot see what you do on your phone.

Sharing

Lockout shares no data with anyone, because it has none to share and no way to send it.

Children

Lockout is a self-control tool for the person who owns the iPhone. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: see the support page.
